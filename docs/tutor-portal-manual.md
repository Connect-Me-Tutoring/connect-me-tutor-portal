**Tutor Portal:** <https://www.connectmego.app/>

This manual walks you through getting set up on the portal, getting paired with a student, and the tools you will use every week.

If you have a question that isn't answered here, use **Get Help** in the portal (see [Getting help](#getting-help)) or contact a Connect Me Board member on Remind or Discord.

## 1. Signing up and logging in

### Sign up

1. Within 1–2 days of joining the Discord, you should receive a sign-up link by email. Check your spam folder too.
2. Open the link and **set your password** (enter it twice to confirm).
3. If you never received the email, contact a Connect Me Board member on Remind or Discord.

### Forgot your password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. You'll receive a **6-digit code** by email. Enter it on the **Enter Verification Code** screen.
4. Once the code is accepted, you'll be taken to a page to set a new password.

> If the code doesn't arrive, wait a minute and request a new one. For security, the portal shows the same message whether or not an account exists for that email.

## 2. Orientation

New tutors complete orientation before the rest of the portal unlocks. Until you finish, **Orientation** is the only item in the sidebar.

Orientation has four modules:

| Module                         | What it covers                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Orientation slideshow**      | Connect Me policies, expectations, and frequently asked questions.                                 |
| **Portal walkthrough**         | A guided practice tour of the portal using sample data. Nothing you do here affects real sessions. |
| **Experienced Tutor Examples** | Real tutoring moments. Identify effective teaching moves and reflect on how you'd use them.        |
| **Knowledge check**            | A short quiz on the most important details.                                                        |

- In the knowledge check, you can retry a question until you get it right.
- You can submit questions for the Operations team when you finish.
- **Finishing the quiz unlocks the full portal.**

You can reopen the portal walkthrough at any time from **Settings → Orientation resources → Reopen portal walkthrough**.

## 3. Getting a student

### Step 1: Complete your profile

Matching is based on your availability, subjects, and languages, so keep these up to date.

1. Open **Profile** in the sidebar. You can also click **Edit profile settings** on the right side of the **Pairings** tab.
2. Add your **availability**, **subjects of interest**, and **languages spoken**.
3. Save your changes.

### Step 2: Join the pairing queue

1. Go to the **Pairings** tab.
2. Under **Pairing Queue**, you can add optional notes about your goals, preferred subjects, or requirements.
3. Turn on **In pairing queue**. While it's on, you can be matched.
4. To pause without losing your saved notes, turn it off. Turn it back on to rejoin with the same preferences.

### Step 3: Accept or decline a match

Students matched to you appear under **Incoming pairings** on the Pairings tab. Each card shows the student's subjects, languages, and availability.

- Click **Accept** if the student is a good fit.
- Click **Decline** if they aren't, for example if your schedules don't line up.

### What happens when you accept

- The portal **automatically creates an enrollment**: a recurring weekly session at a time within both your availability and the student's.
- A **Zoom link** is assigned automatically.
- The parent/guardian is **emailed automatically** with the date, time, and Zoom link.
- If the auto-scheduled time doesn't work, talk with the parent about another time and change it in **My Enrollments** (see [My Enrollments](#my-enrollments)).

### Step 4: Text the parent/guardian

You **must** send a welcome message to your student's parent/guardian to introduce yourself as their tutor.

- Use this [welcome message template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19I-JABrf7u2UHb-v8rZLx4aQ6z9QKaNQ7LCmxtvWODk/edit).
- Parent contact details are on the **My Students** tab.

### Step 5: Read the Starter Packet

Read the [Connect Me Tutor Starter Packet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eVQPX2Rjx-b5n93070zHjXSMpzBs0twwGpRPAOYmHec/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.vrhvb96nxxe9) carefully before your first session. The **Tutor Handbook** on the **Resources** tab is also required reading.

## 4. Your weekly routine

1. **Before the session:** check the **Dashboard** for this week's session time and Zoom link.
2. **Join the session** by clicking **Meeting** on the session, then **Join Meeting**.
3. **After the session:** submit the **Session Exit Form (SEF)** within 48 hours.
4. **If something changes:** reschedule, request a substitute, or cancel from the Dashboard.

### Session Exit Forms (SEFs)

**You only receive volunteer hours for sessions with a submitted SEF.**

- The SEF button unlocks once the session's start time has passed.
- It's due **by 11:59pm EST, two days after the session**. The button counts down for you:
  - _SEF Due MM/DD_ (grey)
  - _SEF Due in 2 days_ (yellow)
  - _SEF Due Tomorrow_ (orange)
  - _SEF Due TODAY by 11:59pm EST_ (red)
  - _SEF Submitted_ (green)

**Filling out the SEF**

- In 2–4 sentences, describe what you covered.
- Check **My student knows about our next class** (required).
- If you have a **question or concern**, check that box, pick a category, and describe it. The categories are:
  - Attendance & Engagement
  - Technical and Portal Issues
  - Student Behavior and Support
  - Urgent Escalation

  The team will follow up with you.

- If the session **didn't happen**, click **The session did not happen** at the top of the form and choose a reason. See [Cancelling](#cancelling-a-session).

**Late or mistaken SEFs**

- **Missed the deadline?** The session is marked _Unconfirmed_, but you can still click **Submit overdue SEF** on the session in **Past Sessions**. Submitting it still earns you the hours.
- **Made a mistake?** Under **Past Sessions**, open the session's actions and click **Undo** to reopen the SEF. You can do this up to 7 days after you submitted it.

### Missing SEFs can end an enrollment

If you stop submitting SEFs for an enrollment, the portal assumes the sessions aren't happening.

| Weeks without a submitted SEF | What happens                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 weeks                       | Warning email, plus a banner on your Dashboard naming the student          |
| 4 weeks                       | Second warning email                                                       |
| 5 weeks                       | **The enrollment and its future sessions are deleted**, and you're emailed |

Submitting overdue SEFs resets this. If you're taking an agreed break, let a board member know.

### Rescheduling a session

To move **one** session, for example just this week's:

1. On the Dashboard, click the **edit** (pencil) icon on the session, or **Actions → Edit** under _Active Sessions_.
2. Pick the new date and time.
3. Choose a **Meeting Link** that's free at that time. Links that are already booked are marked unavailable.
4. Save. An email with the new time and Zoom link goes to the student's email on file. It's still a good idea to text the parent/guardian.

To change the time **every week**, edit the enrollment instead (see [My Enrollments](#my-enrollments)).

### Requesting a substitute

Click the **Request a Substitute** icon (person with a +) on the session. It opens the substitute request form.

### Cancelling a session

1. Click the red **cancel** (calendar ✕) icon on the session, or **Actions → Cancel**.
2. Choose a reason:
   - Student cancelled with prior notice
   - Student Did Not Attend
   - I am cancelling with prior notice
   - Last Minute Emergency
   - Other
3. Add any details and confirm.

Cancelled by mistake? Click **Undo Cancel** (green ✕) on the session.

## 5. Navigating the portal

### Dashboard

Your sessions, split into three sections:

- **This Week:** this week's sessions, with the Zoom link, SEF button, and actions (edit/reschedule, request a substitute, cancel).
- **Active Sessions:** all upcoming sessions.
- **Past Sessions:** completed, cancelled, no-show, and unconfirmed sessions. This is where you submit overdue SEFs, undo a recent SEF, and view your meeting notes.

### Announcements

Important updates from the Connect Me team. Check here regularly.

### My Students

Information about each student you're paired with:

- Start date
- Availability and subjects
- Student email
- Parent/guardian email and phone

**Removing a student:** if a student no longer wants tutoring, click the red trash can, then **Remove Student**.

- This automatically deletes the related enrollments and future sessions. You don't need to delete the enrollments separately.
- Please also let the team know.

### My Enrollments

An **enrollment** is a recurring session with a student: day, time, Zoom link, and how often you meet.

**Edit an enrollment** to change the regular weekly day/time or Zoom link.

- All upcoming sessions on the regular schedule move with it.
- Sessions you've individually rescheduled keep their new time.

**Add an enrollment** if you and your student want an extra weekly session:

1. Click the add button and choose the student.
2. Set the frequency, start date, and day/time.
   - Frequency is **Weekly** or **Biweekly**.
   - Biweekly can only be chosen when creating an enrollment.
   - Pick a time within the student's availability.
3. Pick a Zoom link. A **green dot** means it's available at that time; a **red dot** means it's taken.

**Other tools on this tab:**

- **Copy link:** copies the enrollment's Zoom link to share with the parent/guardian.
- **Chat:** opens a chat for that enrollment.
- **Filters:** narrow the list by name, day, or time.

**One-time extra session?** Don't create a new enrollment for it. Reschedule an existing session, or use **Get Help** so the team can add a one-off session.

**Can't find a free time or Zoom link?** Use **Get Help** (category _Sessions, scheduling, or Zoom links_) or contact a board member.

**Deleting an enrollment** ends that recurring session. If the student is stopping entirely, use **Remove Student** on My Students instead. It cleans up everything at once.

### Chats

Message the Connect Me admin team. Each enrollment also has a chat, opened with the **Chat** button on My Enrollments.

### My Hours

Your volunteer hours:

- **Hours Summary:** session hours, event hours, and total.
- Hours broken down **by student** and **by event**.

Only sessions with a **submitted SEF** (status _Complete_) count toward session hours.

- **Need a signature** for your school or organization's hours form? Fill out the [hours verification request form](https://forms.gle/dqZ822Wj5zeC1eWa8).
- **Hours look wrong?** First check for missing or overdue SEFs on the Dashboard. If they still look wrong, use **Get Help** (category _Volunteer hours_).

### Resources

- The **Tutor Handbook** (read before your first session) and the **Tutor Manual**.
- A searchable table of curated teaching resources: math exercises, reading comprehension, phonics, virtual science labs, writing lessons, and more.

### Worksheets

A searchable library of worksheets and lesson plans hosted on the portal. Filter by category or collection, then preview or download.

### Pairings

Join or leave the pairing queue and accept or decline incoming matches. See [Getting a student](#3-getting-a-student). If you already have a student and would like another, turn on **In pairing queue** again.

### Profile

Edit your availability, subjects of interest, and languages spoken. Keep this current so you're matched with the right students.

### Zoom links

**Always join through the portal.** Click **Meeting** on a session, then **Join Meeting**, or **Copy Meeting Link** to share it. Zoom links are assigned to specific time slots, so don't reuse a link at a different time without rescheduling in the portal first.

## 6. Settings

Open **Settings** from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

- **Profiles:** if your account has more than one profile, switch between them here.
- **Notification Settings:** turn session reminder and webinar reminder emails on or off. Text reminders are still in development.
- **Orientation resources:** reopen the portal walkthrough.
- **Email Address:** change your login email. Confirmation links are sent to both your current and new address, and the change takes effect once both are clicked.
- **Account Settings:** your name, phone number, age, bio, subjects, languages, and account status.

## Getting help

- **Get Help** (in the sidebar menu) opens a support ticket.
  - Pick a category and urgency, then describe what happened.
  - You'll get an emailed copy, and the team follows up by email.
  - Categories: portal bugs; sessions/scheduling/Zoom links; account/login; pairing; volunteer hours; feedback; other.
- **Serious incidents** (safety concerns, harassment, misconduct) go through the **serious incident report form**, linked inside the Get Help window. Don't use a regular ticket for these.
- You can also reach a **Connect Me Board member** on Remind or Discord.
